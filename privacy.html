Privacy policy

How we handle your information

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Finqube Services ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through finqubeservices.com, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable Indian laws.

What we collect

  • Enquiry questionnaire: your name, mobile number, email address, the services you are interested in, your preferred time for a call and anything you choose to write in the message box.
  • Reviews: your name, city or business name (optional), the service you used, your rating and your review.
  • WhatsApp, phone and email: whatever you choose to share with us when you contact us directly.
  • Website statistics: anonymous visit counts, such as pages viewed, device type and country. These statistics do not use cookies and do not identify you.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and contact you at the time you chose.
  • To send you a proposal or fee quote for the services you asked about.
  • To show your review on our website, only after you have agreed to it and we have approved it.
  • To understand which pages are useful so we can improve the website.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not add you to marketing lists without your permission.

Who processes it for us

Form submissions are delivered to our email through Web3Forms, our email is hosted with GoDaddy, the website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and visit statistics are counted by Cloudflare Web Analytics. These providers handle data only to deliver their service. If you message us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp's own privacy policy also applies.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for up to 12 months if you do not become a client, so we can follow up on your request. If you become a client, we keep records for as long as tax and other laws require. Published reviews stay on the website until you ask us to remove them.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Write to us at info@finqubeservices.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Grievances

For any concern about how your data is handled, email info@finqubeservices.com with the subject "Privacy". If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
