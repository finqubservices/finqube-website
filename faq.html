Accounting, bookkeeping & MIS

About this service
What does your bookkeeping service include?

We record your sales, purchases, expenses, receipts and payments in Tally Prime, Zoho Books or QuickBooks, keep ledgers for customers and suppliers, and maintain the GST and TDS details needed for your returns. You get up-to-date books every month instead of a rush at year end.

What do I need to share for bookkeeping?

Usually your bank statements, sales and purchase invoices, expense bills and any loan or investment statements. If you already use accounting software, we can work directly in it with a user login you create for us.

Why are bank and party reconciliations important?

Reconciliation matches your books with your bank statements and with what customers and suppliers say you owe. It catches missed entries, duplicate payments and unpaid invoices early, and it is the base for correct GST input tax credit and accurate profit figures.

What happens in monthly closing?

At month end we record pending bills, accrued expenses, provisions, depreciation and closing stock, then check the trial balance. This gives you a reliable profit and loss figure for each month, not just at year end.

What is an MIS report and do small businesses need one?

MIS (Management Information System) reports are short monthly summaries: sales, profit, expenses, cash position, money to be collected and money to be paid. Small businesses benefit the most, because they show problems such as slow collections or rising costs before they become serious. We tailor the report to the numbers you actually use.

Do you prepare final accounts for audit?

Yes. We prepare the balance sheet, profit and loss statement, schedules and notes at year end, and coordinate with your auditor so the audit runs smoothly. We do not sign audit reports ourselves.

What does payroll processing cover?

We calculate monthly salaries with attendance, deductions, PF, ESI, professional tax and TDS on salary, and prepare payslips and the salary register. We also share the bank upload file so you can pay salaries in one step.

Income tax & TDS

About this service
Who has to file an income tax return?

Generally, anyone whose total income is above the basic exemption limit, and also people who meet certain conditions such as high-value deposits, foreign travel spending or large electricity bills, even if their income is lower. Filing is also needed to claim a refund. Businesses, firms, LLPs and companies must file every year regardless of profit or loss.

What is the last date to file my return?

For the year 2025-26, the due date is 31 July 2026 for most individuals without business income (ITR-1 and ITR-2), 31 August 2026 for business and professional cases that do not need a tax audit, and 31 October 2026 where a tax audit applies. Dates can be extended by the government, so we confirm the current date when we take up your return.

Old tax regime or new tax regime: which is better for me?

The new regime has lower rates but allows very few deductions; it is the default. The old regime allows deductions such as HRA, home loan interest and investments, but has higher rates. We calculate your tax both ways using your actual salary and investments and recommend the one that saves you more.

Do I need to pay advance tax?

If your total tax for the year, after TDS, is ₹10,000 or more, you must pay it in instalments by 15 June, 15 September, 15 December and 15 March. Missing an instalment means interest. Salaried people with income only from salary usually have this covered through TDS. We estimate your liability and remind you before each date.

Have the TDS return forms changed under the new Income-tax Act?

Yes. From 1 April 2026, under the Income-tax Act, 2025, quarterly TDS statements have new form numbers: Form 138 replaces Form 24Q (salary), Form 140 replaces Form 26Q (other payments to residents), Form 144 replaces Form 27Q (payments to non-residents), and Form 143 replaces Form 27EQ for TCS. Salary TDS certificates (earlier Form 16) are now Form 130, and other TDS certificates (earlier Form 16A) are Form 131. We handle the change-over for you.

When are TDS payments and TDS returns due?

TDS deducted in a month is generally paid by the 7th of the next month. Quarterly TDS returns are due by 31 July, 31 October, 31 January and 31 May. Late payment attracts interest and late returns attract a fee for every day of delay, so we keep a calendar and file on time.

How is tax on capital gains calculated?

It depends on what you sold (shares, mutual funds, property, gold) and how long you held it, which decides whether the gain is short-term or long-term. We compute the gain from your purchase and sale records, apply the correct rate, and check exemptions available for reinvesting in a house or specified bonds.

I received an income tax notice. What should I do?

Don't ignore it, and don't panic. Most notices are about a mismatch between your return and the information the department has (for example TDS, interest or high-value transactions). Send us the notice and your return; we work out what it is asking for, prepare the reply or rectification, and file it online before the deadline.

Do NRIs need to file a return in India?

An NRI must file if their income earned in India, such as rent, interest, or capital gains, is above the exemption limit, or to claim a refund of excess TDS. Property sales by NRIs have high TDS, which can often be reduced through a lower deduction certificate applied for in advance. We handle both.

GST compliance

About this service
When is GST registration compulsory?

Broadly, when your annual turnover crosses ₹40 lakh for goods or ₹20 lakh for services (lower limits apply in some special category states). Registration is compulsory regardless of turnover for inter-state sellers of goods, e-commerce sellers and certain other cases. We check your situation and handle the online application.

Which GST returns do I have to file, and when?

Most regular taxpayers file GSTR-1 (sales details) by the 11th and GSTR-3B (summary and payment) by the 20th of the next month. Businesses with turnover up to ₹5 crore can choose the quarterly QRMP scheme, filing returns quarterly while paying tax monthly. Composition dealers follow a separate, simpler system.

Why is my input tax credit lower than my purchase bills?

You can claim credit only for invoices your suppliers have reported in their returns, which appear in your GSTR-2B. If a supplier files late or makes a mistake, your credit is held up. We reconcile your purchase register with GSTR-2B every month and follow up with suppliers so you don't lose credit.

Is the GST annual return compulsory?

GSTR-9 is mandatory for businesses with turnover above ₹2 crore and optional below that. Businesses with turnover above ₹5 crore also file GSTR-9C, a reconciliation statement between the books and the returns. We prepare both from your reconciled books.

Do I need e-invoicing and e-way bills?

E-invoicing currently applies when your annual turnover in any year since 2017-18 has crossed ₹5 crore. An e-way bill is generally needed to move goods worth more than ₹50,000. We set up the process and can generate them for you if needed.

I received a GST notice. Can you help?

Yes. Common notices relate to differences between GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and GSTR-2B, excess credit or late filing. We study the notice, reconcile the figures and prepare a clear reply with supporting documents within the time allowed.

Business setup & consulting

About this service
Should I register a private limited company, an LLP or a proprietorship?

A proprietorship is the simplest and cheapest, but you are personally liable. An LLP gives limited liability with lighter compliance and suits professional and small partnership businesses. A private limited company suits businesses planning to raise investment or grow large, but has more annual filings. We explain the costs and compliance of each for your situation before you decide.

What do I need to register a company or LLP?

At least two directors or partners, their PAN, Aadhaar, photos and address proof, a registered office address with a utility bill and owner's NOC, and the proposed name. Digital signatures are obtained as part of the process. Registration usually takes a few weeks, depending on name approval and government processing.

What is Udyam registration and is it worth it?

Udyam is the free government registration for micro, small and medium enterprises. It helps with priority-sector bank loans, some government schemes and tenders, and protection against delayed payments from large buyers. We also handle other licences such as Shops & Establishment, professional tax and the Import Export Code.

When do PF and ESI apply to my business?

PF generally applies once you have 20 or more employees, and ESI once you have 10 or more employees in covered areas, with ESI covering employees earning up to ₹21,000 a month. After registration, contributions and returns are due every month. We handle registration and monthly filing.

What annual ROC filings does a company or LLP have?

Every company files its financial statements (AOC-4) and annual return (MGT-7 or MGT-7A) with the Registrar each year, and holds a board meeting and AGM. LLPs file Form 11 (annual return) and Form 8 (statement of accounts). Late filing attracts heavy daily fees, so we track all dates.

What is a project report or CMA data, and when do I need it?

Banks ask for a project report and CMA (Credit Monitoring Arrangement) data when you apply for a term loan or working capital limit. It sets out your business plan, projected sales, profits, cash flows and repayment capacity. We prepare realistic projections that match your past numbers and the bank's format.

What is a compliance calendar?

It is a single list of every filing your business needs in the year: GST, TDS, advance tax, PF, ESI, ROC and income tax, with dates. We prepare it for you at the start and remind you before each date, so nothing is missed.
